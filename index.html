OH. HELLO THERE.

We're Look Behind You Software, an independent Scottish startup with a fairly straightforward philosophy: notice annoying things, then build something about them.

Some ideas might become big. Some might help fifty people. If fifty people's lives are slightly less annoying because of something we made, that's still pretty cool.

We grew up waiting for games to load from cassette tapes, asking Jeeves things, burning questionable mix CDs and being told not to believe everything we read on the internet.

Look how that last one turned out.
