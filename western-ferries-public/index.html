Western Ferries Checker

Is the Dunoon ferry running? There's an app for that.

You're at the terminal, the sky is doing its usual thing, and someone says "I think it's running." That is not a travel plan. Western Ferries Checker shows you what the Dunoon to Gourock service is actually doing, so you can stop guessing and start walking.

Get it on Google Play iOS coming soon.

What it does

  • Live status. Whether the service is running normally, partly disrupted or suspended, at a glance.
  • Timetable and next departures. What's due to leave next, without hunting for a PDF.
  • Alerts. Switch on notifications and the app tells you when the service changes to amber or red.
  • Community queue reports. Other travellers can report how long the queue is at Dunoon or Gourock. Handy, but these come from app users, not from Western Ferries.
  • Support Local. A small section for local businesses that back the app.

What it doesn't do

  • Make you sign up, log in or hand over your name, email address or location.
  • Sell your data. Ever.
  • Pretend to be official. It is an independent app and has no connection to Western Ferries.

The small print, but friendly. Western Ferries Checker is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Western Ferries Ltd. It simply makes publicly available service information easier to check. If the ferry company says one thing and the app says another, believe the ferry company. They own the boats.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, and it shows ads provided by Google AdMob to help cover the costs. In the UK and the European Economic Area you are asked for your permission before personalised ads are shown.

Privacy

There are no accounts, and only a few anonymous bits of information are used to run alerts and queue reports. The full, grown-up version is in our Privacy Policy.
